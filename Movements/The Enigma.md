The Enigma is a movement which gives the illusion that the [[Contact Sphere]] stays still even as it rolls.  It is used both in [[Isolations]] and to create movement in the sphere that feels unexpected relative to the movement of the fingers. 

The [[Contact Sphere]] is held between two contact points, say the thumb of one hand and the finger of the other and then rolls in the hands as it transitions to the symmetrically opposite position in the hands. 

Different patterns enable you to move the sphere in any plane and combine them to trace arbitrary shapes in space in this mysterious way.  

By synchronizing the movement of the hand with that of the sphere, it can create an Isolation, breaking the expectation of the viewer that this motion of the hands will produce movement in the prop.
